# Confirmed Business Decisions

Status: `APPROVED_FOR_DESIGN`

## Organization and GST

| Decision | Confirmed rule |
|---|---|
| Organization | Single current organization: Zitaware Solutions |
| Legal entities | Schema supports 3–4; initial UI remains simple |
| GST registrations | One current registration; schema supports multiple per legal entity |
| Entity ownership | Every posted financial document belongs to exactly one legal entity |
| GST ownership | Every taxable posted document references the issuing/receiving GST registration |

## Project

### Project stages

1. `WON`
2. `WORK_ORDER_RECEIVED`
3. `WAITING_FOR_PERMISSIONS`
4. `WORK_STARTED`
5. `WORK_DONE`
6. `SUCCESSFULLY_COMPLETED`

### Project operational statuses

- `ACTIVE`
- `ON_HOLD`
- `CANCELLED`
- `CLOSED`

Stage and status are independent. A project may be at `WORK_STARTED` and operationally `ON_HOLD`.

### Lead and Tender

- Lead and Tender are separate upstream records.
- A Project may reference a Lead, Tender, or both.
- Neither Lead nor Tender is forced to contain the other lifecycle.
- EMD/SD may reference Tender, Project, or both as the work progresses.

## Milestone, invoice, and receipt

### CAPEX

- CAPEX milestones are event-based.
- A CAPEX milestone may be billed through one or multiple invoices.
- Total finalized invoice value may exceed the approved milestone value (CAPEX) or the installment amount (OPEX); amounts change in practice. The Finalize dialog shows how much more is billed, and remaining amounts never go below zero (ADR-165, replacing the earlier cap).

### OPEX

- One OPEX milestone represents an item over a service duration.
- Saving it generates periodic installment/schedule rows.
- Frequency may be monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
- A schedule row creates an alert; it does not create an invoice or journal.
- Each due period is manually invoiced.
- A normal OPEX installment has one active finalized invoice; a voided invoice may be replaced.
- Rounding difference is placed in the final installment so the schedule total equals the approved OPEX total exactly.

### Invoice

- Invoice is generated by the CRM.
- Every invoice belongs to exactly one project and one milestone.
- An OPEX invoice also references one installment.
- One milestone can have multiple invoices.
- Invoice number is controlled by GST registration and financial year.
- Draft invoices are editable.
- Finalized invoices are immutable.

### Receipt

- One client remittance may settle multiple invoices.
- One invoice may receive multiple receipts.
- Actual bank/cash amount is recorded separately from invoice amount settled.
- TDS, GST-TDS, hold, retention, penalties, and other deductions are separately classified and lifecycle-tracked.
- A hold release references the original hold deduction.

## Accounting

- Proper double-entry accounting.
- Accrual basis.
- Explicit debit and credit journal lines.
- Backend posting engine is the only source of accounting calculations.
- Posted corrections use reversal, credit note, debit note, or replacement.
- Accounting periods may be locked.

## Procurement

- Canonical flow (revised Phase 6.4 — ADR-079; Purchase Request merged into Vendor Bill):

  `Vendor Bill (raised directly with vendor/project/GST/line detail, approved against the real invoice) → Payment Request → Vendor Payment → Bank Reconciliation`

  Purchase Request no longer exists as a separate pre-bill estimate/approval document — a Vendor Bill is now raised directly (vendor, project, GST registration, and line items entered against the real invoice in hand) and carries its own approval gate (draft → submit → approve, where approve both makes the maker-checker decision and posts the ledger entry in one action). Optional PO and Goods/Service Receipt remain unbuilt and are no longer named as intermediate steps in this chain.
- Accountant may approve vendor bills and payment requests.
- Assigned Project Manager may approve vendor bills and payment requests for their assigned project.
- Request creator cannot approve their own request (bill or payment request) unless they hold the dedicated self-approval permission and supply a mandatory justification note.
- There are no amount-based approval slabs.
- A lightweight direct-expense path remains available for eligible small/non-procurement expenses.

## Subscription

- One simple subscription master with basic details and frequency.
- Frequency creates reminders/cycle forecasts.
- A reminder does not automatically post expense.
- Actual expense/vendor bill is recorded when confirmed.

## Employee costing

- Employee and System User are separate entities.
- Optional employee-to-user link.
- Payroll processing is out of scope.
- Monthly gross salary is imported.
- Effort and Contribution become one monthly allocation workflow.
- Allocation must total 100%, including non-project allocation targets.

Formula:

`Project salary cost = imported monthly gross salary × project allocation percentage`

## Inventory

- One central inventory location.
- Authorized users add stock entries.
- Stock receipts link to the relevant approved purchase/vendor bill line when applicable.
- No fixed-asset or depreciation module.
- Stock-tracked items use Inventory; non-stock goods/services use Expense.

## Introducer

- Advances and overpayments remain supported.
- Agreement/accrual, payment, allocation, and unallocated advance are separate concepts.
- TDS and challan/certificate references remain trackable.

## UI

- Existing HTML is authoritative for intended UI structure and field discovery.
- Minor UI changes are allowed when required for correct accounting, tax, validation, permissions, or reconciliation.
- HTML sample calculations are not authoritative.
